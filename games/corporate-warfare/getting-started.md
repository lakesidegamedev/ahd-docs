# Getting started

## Download

Corporate Warfare is free while in development. There is no account and no sign-in.

## Install

- **Windows.** Run the .msi installer. Windows may show a SmartScreen warning for a new publisher; choose More info, then Run anyway. The installer adds a Start menu entry and a desktop shortcut and lets you pick the install folder.
- **Linux.** Install the .deb with your package manager, for example `sudo apt install ./corporate-warfare_*.deb`.
- **Android.** The APK installs outside the Play Store. Allow installs from your browser or file manager when your phone asks.

## Offline play and saves

The game is single-player and plays fully offline. Saves stay on your device. Use Save in the in-game menu to keep a save beside the autosave, and Load game to pick any save; each one shows its company, game week and date.

At launch the game can check the public release list for a newer version (you can switch this off in Settings). In Settings, Updates you choose between full releases and previews, and you can install any version from 1.8 preview 3 on; going back to an older version backs up your saves first. On Windows, Update now downloads and installs the new version and reopens the game. On Linux, Mac and Android it opens the download.

The game can also send an anonymous market snapshot every five game weeks: prices, sales and stock per good, with a random install id and nothing about you. It never stops play if it fails, and the Analytics switch in Settings turns it off.

## Your first career

The game opens on the main menu: Continue, New game, Load game, Settings and, on desktop, Quit.

New game walks you through:

1. **Scenario.** Pick one of the [four scenarios](scenarios.md), or a custom scenario you made in the scenario editor.
2. **How you start.** Take over a running company, or found your own: name it, pick a headquarters region, a first strategy by sector and how much capital to put in.
3. **Difficulty.** Easy gives you more money and gentler rivals; Hard gives you less and every rival fights for share.
4. **The economy on day one** and your starting capital.
5. **World price path.** Historical (the real shape on the real dates), Historical with variance (the same shape, with the timing and depth of each swing shifted by your game's seed) or Random. Each scenario sets a default.

A guided tutorial of eight steps ticks off as you do them, each with a Show me button. You can restart it from Settings.

## The screens

- **Map.** A globe that flattens onto the scenario as you zoom in. It shows your divisions, room to build, wages and the freight network. Tap a region for its board: operators, market shares, rivals you can raid and plants.
- **CEO Office.** Your company's results, where the quarter's money went, pricing for every good, your divisions and plants.
- **Markets.** The stock exchange, the commodity board, the bond list and the economy card: the business cycle, the central bank, the government and the laws in force.
- **Inbox.** Decisions that need you, then the news.
- **Portfolio.** Your net worth, your companies and your shareholdings.
- **Command Center.** Every action in the game in one searchable list, each with a form, a live preview and Submit.

On desktop: Space plays or pauses, N plays the next week, 1, 2 and 3 set the speed, F1 to F7 switch screens and Ctrl+K opens the Command Center. On Mac, Command K, Command N and Command P do the same.
