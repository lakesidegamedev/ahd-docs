# Changelog

The full release history, including what is coming in the next release, is in the game's changelog:

https://github.com/lakesidegamedev/corporate-warfare/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md

Every release lists what was added, changed and fixed, and says when saves from an earlier version will not load. The latest release is shown on the download page:

https://lakesidegames.net/download/corporate-warfare/

## Recent releases

- **1.8.0 (2026-10-29).** True 3D voxel buildings and vehicles on the real Earth, trucks and trains on real interstates and railways, full company financials with customizable charts, deeper market, region, division, freight and rival views, a choice between full releases and previews with any version from 1.8 preview 3 on installable, faster weeks and an 8-bit interface. Careers saved in 1.7.1 and the 1.8 previews load in 1.8.0.
- **1.7.1 (2026-10-28).** Fixes and depth from the first week of Lone Star: no instant raids on new players, company pages with logos and a face for every CEO, readable freight, a CFO who says why you are losing money, and every screen on iPhone, iPad and Android. Saves from 1.7.0 do not load.
- **1.7.0, Lone Star (2026-10-27).** Supply chains you can own and contest, a shipping network, deeper corporate play, rivals with names and memories, and three new playable scenarios: Texas Oil Patch 1981, Silicon Valley 1995 and Ruhr and Rhine 1991 with currencies.
- **1.6.1 (2026-10-27).** Android and desktop fixes from the first tester reports.
- **1.6.0 (2026-10-27).** Business cycles, a central bank, governments and laws; a globe main menu, scenario picker, difficulty and starting economy; the Command Center; a desktop layout; load game; a tutorial and in-game updates.
- **1.5.0 (2026-10-27).** The whole world on a globe, scenarios, a Stats screen and tougher rivals.
- **1.4.0 (2026-10-26).** The first Android, Windows and Linux builds.

Last checked: 2026-10-29, version 1.8.0
