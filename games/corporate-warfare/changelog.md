# Changelog

The full release history, including what is coming in the next release, is in the game's changelog:

https://github.com/lakesidegamedev/corporate-warfare/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md

Every release lists what was added, changed and fixed, and says when saves from an earlier version will not load. The Lakeside launcher keeps the game up to date, and the download page shows the current version:

https://lakesidegames.net/download/corporate-warfare/

## Recent releases

- **2.0.2 (2026-11-13).** The Mac game can update itself from inside the game. Double-tap the map to zoom. Sortable, saveable tables on iPad and Mac. Android back button and end of career fixes.
- **2.0.1 (2026-11-13).** Update the paid game from inside the game by signing in to Lakeside.
- **2.0.0 (2026-11-13).** The biggest change since the first release. Careers with a start, an ambition and an ending. Rivals that build, fail and raid on the same rules you do, with raids announced four weeks ahead. Prices that move for reasons you can read. Plants of failed companies auctioned. A new table layout on desktop and phone, the 3D map as the default map everywhere, rebindable keys, a What's new panel, and faster weeks. Saves from every earlier version still open.
- **1.8.0 (2026-10-29).** True 3D buildings and vehicles on the real Earth, full company financials with customizable charts, deeper market, region, division, freight and rival views, and faster weeks.
- **1.7.0, Lone Star (2026-10-27).** Supply chains you can own and contest, a shipping network, deeper corporate play, rivals with names and memories, and three new playable scenarios: Texas Oil Patch 1981, Silicon Valley 1995 and Ruhr and Rhine 1991 with currencies.
- **1.6.0 (2026-10-27).** Business cycles, a central bank, governments and laws; a globe main menu, scenario picker, difficulty and starting economy; the Command Center; a desktop layout; a tutorial.
- **1.5.0 (2026-10-27).** The whole world on a globe, scenarios, a Stats screen and tougher rivals.
- **1.4.0 (2026-10-26).** The first Android, Windows and Linux builds.

Last checked: 2026-11-14, version 2.0.2
