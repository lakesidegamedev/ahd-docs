# Changelog

The full release history, including what is coming in the next release, is in the game's changelog:

https://github.com/lakesidegamedev/corporate-warfare/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md

Every release lists what was added, changed and fixed, and says when saves from an earlier version will not load. The latest release is shown on the download page:

https://lakesidegames.net/download/corporate-warfare/

## Recent releases

- **1.7.0, Lone Star.** Supply chains you can own and contest, a shipping network, deeper corporate play, rivals with names and memories, and three new playable scenarios: Texas Oil Patch 1981, Silicon Valley 1995 and Ruhr and Rhine 1991 with currencies.
- **1.6.0.** Business cycles, a central bank, governments and laws; a globe main menu, scenario picker, difficulty and starting economy; the Command Center; a desktop layout; load game; a tutorial and in-game updates.
- **1.5.0.** The whole world on a globe, scenarios, a Stats screen and tougher rivals.
- **1.4.0.** The first Android, Windows and Linux builds.
