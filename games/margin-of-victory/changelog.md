# Changelog

The full release history is in the game's changelog:

https://github.com/lakesidegamedev/MOVGame/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md

Each release lists what was added, changed and fixed. Margin of Victory had no version numbers until 2.0.0, so earlier history is grouped by month.

## Recent releases

- **2.0.0 (2026-11-12).** The game grows from six countries to fourteen, with 131 elections in the store catalog. Eight new countries: New Zealand, India, Japan, Brazil, Ireland, Spain, Italy and Mexico. The United States now reaches back to 1932, Canada to 1984, Germany to 1990, France to 1981 and Australia to 1996. Every country has one free election, and the store sells each country as a pack or everything as the Complete Collection. Difficulty means the same thing in every country. Every election has its own issues and real story beats in the weeks they happened. Ten voting systems are explained in a new Voting Systems tab. Leaders have photos and parties have logos. The web, desktop, iPhone and Android versions have a new interface.
- **Before 2.0.** The game was called Electioneer until September 2026 and covered six countries and 49 elections.

Last checked: 2026-11-14, version 2.0
